# The Business of Data

A graduate course on the financial, legal, and organizational decisions that
determine whether a model becomes a product. Taught by case study rather than
code.

Fourteen weeks. Each week pairs a 90-minute asynchronous module on one business
function with a 90-minute synchronous case discussion, in which four students
hold conflicting roles and account for a decision real managers made.

Site: https://johnsanterre.github.io/business-of-data/

## Status

Draft. Weeks 1 to 4 are detailed; weeks 5 to 14 are outlined.

**Week 2** has full materials: a case packet, exhibits, role briefs, a reference
note, two background research reports, and six rendered lectures.

**Week 3** has the complete asynchronous module — six scripts, six decks,
narration, and six rendered lectures — plus the reference note on liquidity,
solvency, and runway. The case materials wait on the background research report;
the prompt for it is at `materials/w03/research-prompt.txt`.

The two tracks are independent by design. An async module teaches the concepts
and never names the week's case, so it can be built before the case research
exists. Every script carries that constraint in its front matter.

Figures in the week 2 background research are marked as filed, reported, or not
established, and the not-established items have not been verified against primary
sources. Every figure spoken in the week 3 lectures is computed in
`materials/w03/scripts/w03-numbers.py`, with the output preserved beside it.
