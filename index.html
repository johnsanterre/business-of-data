Assessment

Grading

ComponentWeightDescription
Panel sessions 30% Each student is assigned a role in three sessions. Assessment covers command of the material, whether the position held up under questioning, and whether the student conceded points that were well made against them.
Written work 30% Each student submits a position paper before an assigned session and a synthesis after it, and three written questions in each of the nine weeks they are not assigned a role.
Final project 40% Groups of four write a teaching case from a company one member has access to, with exhibits and a teaching note, and present it in week 14.

There is no examination. Final project checkpoints fall in weeks 3, 5, 9, and 12.

Policies

Course policies

Attendance
Synchronous sessions are required.
Recording
Synchronous sessions are recorded and made available to enrolled students.
Use of AI tools
Permitted for all written work. Students are responsible for the content of what they submit and must be able to explain any statement in it.
Confidentiality in the final project
Anonymize by default. Named employers require written permission from someone with authority to give it. Material under a non-disclosure agreement may not be used, whether anonymized or not.
Late work
Questions submitted after a session has begun receive no credit, since their purpose is to shape the discussion. Other written work is accepted late with a deduction.
Academic integrity and accommodations
University policy applies. Students registered with the Disabled Students' Program should contact the instructor in the first two weeks.
