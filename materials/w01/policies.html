Policies

Course policies

Assessment, attendance, recording, confidentiality.

Assessment

There is no exam. Three instruments, and sixty per cent of the grade is live work in the room on weeks you cannot outsource.

InstrumentWeightWhat it measures
Panel weeks, three at ten per cent30% Defending a real decision under questioning
Questions and participation, eleven weeks30% Preparation on the weeks you are not on the panel
Final case and presentation40% Whether the frames transferred to new material

Panel weeks

Graded per session on whether the role was occupied rather than described, whether the position survived questioning, and whether you knew the material well enough to concede a good point instead of deflecting it. Conceding well scores.

Questions and participation

Three written questions per non-panel week, submitted before class, answerable from the room's packet. Scored lightly and quickly. The follow-up in the room carries the rest of this weight: submitting sharp questions and never speaking is half the assignment.

The panel guide sets out what separates a question worth the grade from one worth nothing.

The final

In groups of four, sourced by the group, presented in week 14. Named ownership of each component and a confidential peer evaluation applied as a multiplier on the group mark. Full requirements are in the final project brief.

Attendance

The live session is the course. Three of your weeks are panel weeks and the other eleven carry the participation weight, so absence is expensive by construction rather than by penalty.

Missing a panel week
One empty seat breaks a session, so the rule is fixed rather than negotiated in the moment. The instructor covers the role for that session. Your makeup is to write the role's full position paper and to open the following week's class with it.
Missing a non-panel week
The three written questions are still due before the session. They carry part of the weight on their own; the participation half cannot be made up.

Recording and session conduct

Sessions are recorded
All of them. This matters most before the week 5 final-project pitches, because a student who forgets it may say something about an employer that cannot be unsaid.
Cameras on
Panel and room alike. Cold call does not work against a wall of initials, and the cross-examination format depends on it.
Panelists rename to Name — Role
The room needs to see who it is talking to.
Questions queue in chat
The instructor calls them. This solves turn-taking better in video than it is ever solved in person.

Confidentiality

These rules apply to the final project and to anything you say in the room about your own employer. Read them before you start reporting, not when the draft is written.

Anonymise by default
"A mid-size health insurer" carries the case as well as the name does. The teaching value is in the decision, not the logo.
Named employers need written permission
From somebody with authority to give it, reproduced as one email in the appendix.
Nothing under NDA, ever, anonymised or not
Anonymisation is not a legal defence and you should not treat it as one.
What is said in the room stays in the room
Cases in this course involve real companies and, in the final projects, real employers of your classmates. Do not repeat outside the session what a classmate says about their own workplace inside it.

Use of AI tools

To be set before the term begins. The decision this policy has to make: whether an AI tool may be used to prepare a panel role and to draft the final case, and what must be disclosed when it is. The two constraints already fixed by the course design are that panel weeks are graded live under questioning, where a tool cannot sit in for you, and that the final case is scored on access to primary material that no tool can obtain.

Late work

To be set before the term begins. The decision this policy has to make: the penalty for the three weekly questions, which lose most of their value once the session has run, and the penalty for the final case, which has a fixed presentation date in week 14. The week 5 access pitch has no useful late form, since its purpose is to catch an unworkable project early enough to change it.

Academic integrity and accommodations

To be set before the term begins, and to follow Berkeley's standard language rather than anything course-specific.
