SEGMENT 4 — CONCENTRATION
==================================================================
A book of 400 equal loans. Diversification depends on correlation, not count.
  pairwise correlation 0.00 -> behaves like  400.00 independent loans
  pairwise correlation 0.05 -> behaves like   19.09 independent loans
  pairwise correlation 0.10 -> behaves like    9.78 independent loans
  pairwise correlation 0.25 -> behaves like    3.97 independent loans
  pairwise correlation 0.50 -> behaves like    2.00 independent loans

The floor, stated as volatility rather than as a count.
sd of the book average, as a fraction of one loan's sd:
  correlation 0.00 ->  0.050  (  5.0 per cent of a single loan)
  correlation 0.05 ->  0.229  ( 22.9 per cent of a single loan)
  correlation 0.10 ->  0.320  ( 32.0 per cent of a single loan)
  correlation 0.25 ->  0.502  ( 50.2 per cent of a single loan)
  the 0.25 book is 10.0 times as volatile as the independent book of the same size

Herfindahl on an unequal book (share of total exposure):
  looks concentrated: H = 0.2650, effective names =    3.8
  looks diversified : H = 0.0025, effective names =  400.0

  The 400-name book scores 400 effective names on Herfindahl,
  which counts only size. At correlation 0.25 it behaves like 4.0.
  Herfindahl sees the size distribution and is blind to the common cause.
